2. OpenAI fast-tracks AI agent phone to first half 2027 with enhanced vision capabilities
   OpenAI is accelerating its first AI phone to mass production in H1 2027—a full year earlier than previously reported, according to supply chain analyst Ming-Chi Kuo. The device features an enhanced image signal processor with advanced HDR for improved real-world visual sensing, dual AI processors for vision and language tasks, and MediaTek as the sole chip supplier. Kuo projects combined 2027–28 shipments could reach 30M units if development stays on track, with the timeline shift attributed to IPO ambitions and rising competition in AI-native phones.
   Sources: The Rundown — 📱 OpenAI locking in 2027 for its AI phone

3. OpenAI introduces GPT-Realtime-2 with reasoning, tool use, and natural voice interaction
   OpenAI released GPT-Realtime-2, GPT-Realtime-Translate, and GPT-Realtime-Whisper—three API voice models bringing GPT-5-level reasoning to live speech with the ability to use multiple tools simultaneously and speak while thinking. On Big Bench Audio, Realtime-2 achieved 96.6% accuracy versus 81.4% for its predecessor, a 15-point improvement. The release includes a live translator for 70+ languages and streaming transcription, with Zillow, Priceline, and Deutsche Telekom already building voice agents on the platform.
   Sources: The Rundown — 🗣️ OpenAI closes reasoning gap in voice agents

4. Anthropic launches 10 pre-built AI agents for financial services and insurance workflows
   Anthropic unveiled 10 ready-to-run AI agents designed for financial services, capable of handling pitchbook creation, KYC file screening, earnings review, and valuation work. Each agent includes domain-specific skills, data source connectors, and Claude model integration; firms can adapt agents to their own modeling conventions and approval workflows. Claude is also gaining a Microsoft 365 add-in and data connectors from Dun & Bradstreet, Verisk, and IBISWorld, reinforcing Anthropic's domain-by-domain enterprise strategy.
   Sources: The Rundown — 📱 OpenAI locking in 2027 for its AI phone

5. Peter Thiel leads $140M Series B for Panthalassa floating AI data centers powered by ocean waves
   PayPal and Palantir founder Peter Thiel led a $140M Series B for Oregon-based Panthalassa, an autonomous floating compute startup valued at nearly $1B. The company builds 85-meter steel nodes that convert wave motion into electricity, with onboard AI chips cooled by seawater and results beamed via SpaceX's Starlink. The funding will complete a pilot factory near Portland and deploy first commercial nodes in the Pacific in 2027. The project addresses growing public hostility toward data center construction on land by offering an ocean-based alternative.
   Sources: The Rundown — 🌊 AI data centers head for the ocean
